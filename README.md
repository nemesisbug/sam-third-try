## The entry

> On his third try, Sam read the whole paragraph aloud without stopping, and the classroom held its breath until the last word.

One line, 125 characters. A boy who stumbles over reading aloud finally gets
through a whole paragraph in front of his class — no trophy, no applause, just
a room going quiet until the last word lands. The pride is earned inside the
line: two failed tries before it, and a whole classroom holding its breath.
The line is written by the program, not pasted in by hand: `main.py` holds the
text as a constant and checks it against the 1–240 character gate before
writing it out.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the line to
`/output/entry.txt`. No network, no input files, no secrets.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
